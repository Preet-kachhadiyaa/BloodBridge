---
## Blood Bridge - Short Workflow
1. Load & Clean Data (3 datasets: donors, blood banks, cities)
2. Map donors/blood banks to cities with lat/lng coordinates
3. Train XGBoost Model on Donor Data
4. Calculate AI Scores & Risk Levels for all donors
5. Show pages for Home, Nearby Hospitals, Nearby Donors, AI Analytics

---
## AI Score Explained
- AI Score is a percentage (0-100%) indicating how likely a donor is eligible to donate blood
- Calculated by the trained XGBoost Classifier model
- Uses 5 donor features to make prediction:
  1. Age
  2. Gender
  3. Blood Group
  4. City
  5. Days Since Last Donation
- Model outputs a probability (0-1), multiplied by 100 to get AI Score (0-100)
- Higher AI Score = more confident model is that the donor is eligible!

---
## Risk Levels Explained
- LOW Risk: AI Score ≥80% → Very confident donor is eligible
- MEDIUM Risk: 60-79% AI Score → Moderately confident
- HIGH Risk: <60% AI Score → Low confidence

---
## Model Used
- **XGBoost Classifier** (Extreme Gradient Boosting)
- Features: Age, Gender, Blood Group, City, Days Since Last Donation
- Target: Donor Eligibility (eligible_for_donation)
- Performance Metrics: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score

---
## Data Overview
- **blood_donation.csv**: Donor info (name, age, gender, blood group, location, eligibility, last donation date)
- **blood_bank_data.csv**: Blood bank info (name, location, contact, category)
- **india_real_cities.csv**: Indian cities with latitude & longitude coordinates

---
## Data Persistence
Important: 
- Original CSV files are **not modified**
- AI scores/predictions are only calculated in memory
- No donor/hospital results are saved to disk/database

## Model Persistence
- Trained XGBoost model + label encoders are saved to `model.pkl` in the project folder after first training
- Subsequent app runs load `model.pkl` instead of retraining
- To retrain model, delete `model.pkl` and restart the app
